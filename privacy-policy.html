Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16th November 2026

Ghost Circuit (the “App”) is a one-thumb arcade game for Android. It works offline and has no account of its own, no ads and no purchases. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. We respect your privacy: the App does not collect, share or sell your personal information and has no analytics. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data We Collect

  • No personal data: The App does not ask for, and does not collect, your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information.
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in: You can sign in to Google Play Games from the Options screen. Sign-in is handled by Google; we never see your email address or password.
  • No analytics or crash reporting: The App does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, Unity Analytics or any other analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No advertising: The App does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.
  • Permissions: The App requests the Android internet permission only for Google Play Games (sign-in, cloud backup and achievements), and vibration for the optional haptics setting. It does not connect to any server run by us.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Game progress only: To remember where you are, the App saves a few small settings in its private storage on your device: which levels you have unlocked, your best number of attempts for each level, your sound, pace, control and accessibility settings, play counts (crashes, near misses, time played), and whether you have seen the intro story.
  • Stays on your device unless you sign in: This information is not sent to us. It is sent to Google Play Games only if you choose to sign in, as described below. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.

Google Play Games (Optional)

  • What is sent: If you sign in, the App sends a backup of your game progress (unlocked levels, best attempts per level, play counts and settings flags) to your Google Play Games account so it follows you to a new device, and records the achievements you earn.
  • Who handles it: This data is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games Services terms. We cannot access it.
  • Your control: If you never sign in, nothing is sent anywhere. You can delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Games Services: The App includes Google’s Play Games Services SDK for the optional sign-in, backup and achievements. It contains no advertising, analytics or other data-collecting SDKs. It is built with the Unity game engine; the App does not enable Unity’s analytics, advertising or purchasing services.
  • Google Play: You download the App through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive that information.

Children’s Privacy

  • Audience: The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.
  • No data from children: Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no information is collected from children either.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your data: We hold no personal information about you. Data you chose to back up lives in your Google account and can be deleted from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. Local data is removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.
  • Policy updates: If the App ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy before that version is released. The current version will always be available at this page, and the Effective Date above will change.
