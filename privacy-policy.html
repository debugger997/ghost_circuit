Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30th October 2026

Ghost Circuit (the “App”) is a one-thumb arcade game for Android. It works completely offline and has no accounts, no ads and no purchases. We respect your privacy: the App does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data We Collect

  • No personal data: The App does not ask for, and does not collect, your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information.
  • No accounts or sign-in: There is no login, registration or Google Sign-In in the App.
  • No analytics or crash reporting: The App does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, Unity Analytics or any other analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No advertising: The App does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.
  • No internet access: The App does not request the Android internet permission and does not connect to any server. It requests no Android permissions at all.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Game progress only: To remember where you are, the App saves a few small settings in its private storage on your device: which levels you have unlocked, your best number of attempts for each level, whether sound is on or off, and whether you have seen the intro story.
  • It never leaves your device: This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.

Third-Party Services

  • No data-collecting SDKs: The App contains no third-party software that collects data. It is built with the Unity game engine; the App does not enable Unity’s analytics, advertising or purchasing services.
  • Google Play: You download the App through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive that information.

Children’s Privacy

  • Audience: The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.
  • No data from children: Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no information is collected from children either.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Nothing to access or delete: We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.
  • Policy updates: If the App ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example by adding an online feature), we will update this policy before that version is released. The current version will always be available at this page, and the Effective Date above will change.
